# Claude Workshop Materials

**Bok Center for Teaching and Learning · Harvard University**

A four-day faculty workshop on using Claude for teaching, course development, and research. These materials are organized into three sections — a reference encyclopedia, hands-on activities, and a gallery of larger projects.

## How to move through this site

**New to AI tools?** Start in the [Encyclopedia → Get Started]({{ site.baseurl }}/encyclopedia/get-started/) and work through the setup guides before anything else.

**Want to build intuitions actively?** Go to [Activities]({{ site.baseurl }}/activities/) for short exercises you can try immediately in Claude.ai or Claude Code.

**Looking for project inspiration?** The [Gallery]({{ site.baseurl }}/gallery/) shows what a few hours of workshop time can produce — from auto-generated makeup exams to interactive physics simulations.
