# Activating Your Harvard Edu Claude Account

Please see below for instructions on getting your Harvard Edu Enterprise account for Claude.

## Step 1: Access Your Claude Account

1. Navigate to [https://claude.ai/](https://claude.ai/join/org/JFoeB08wdedgSElNwH3T2w)
2. Enter your official Harvard Key email. Once you type in your email, the option will appear to "Continue with SSO".

![Animated screen recording: entering a Harvard email at claude.ai and selecting Continue with SSO](https://files.slack.com/files-pri/T0HTW3H0V-F0B6Q4UVBN2/screenrecording2026-05-28at11.09.28am-ezgif.com-video-to-gif-converter.gif?pub_secret=4feb32f061)

3. You might be prompted to download Claude Code or Cowork and answer some questions about how you will use the tool. We recommend skipping these options for now. At this point, you will be ready to use Claude in your web browser.

To confirm you are logged in, you will see "Harvard Edu for FAS/SEAS"

![Claude.ai showing the Harvard Edu for FAS/SEAS workspace label in the lower-left of the sidebar](https://files.slack.com/files-pri/T0HTW3H0V-F0B6VRS4T5J/harvard-edu-claude.png?pub_secret=7e5eeb048c)

![Detail of the Claude.ai sidebar with the Harvard Edu workspace selected](https://files.slack.com/files-pri/T0HTW3H0V-F0B6APL7R55/harvard-edu-claude-2.png?pub_secret=c2795f26b3)

## Step 2: Download Claude Desktop App

In order to use this app, you will need to download the software and login with your account above once again. This is repetitive, but will ensure your app and your web browser version of Claude are properly connected.

1. Navigate to [https://claude.ai/download](https://claude.ai/download).
2. Click "macOS" or "Windows" to download the installer. (System requirements: macOS 11 / Big Sur or higher; Windows 10 or higher.)

![Screenshot of claude.ai/download with macOS and Windows download buttons](https://files.slack.com/files-pri/T0HTW3H0V-F0B8706CVM5/screenshot_2026-06-05_at_3.31.42___pm.png?pub_secret=7128e2c226)

3. Open the downloaded file:
   1. Mac: Open the .dmg and drag the Claude icon into your Applications folder.
   2. PC: Run the .exe installer and follow the prompts.
      1. If prompted to turn on developer mode, please use this link: [https://api.anthropic.com/api/desktop/win32/x64/msix/latest/redirect](https://api.anthropic.com/api/desktop/win32/x64/msix/latest/redirect)
4. Launch Claude from your Applications folder (Mac) or Start menu (PC).

## Step 3: Sign In to the Desktop App

1. Open the Claude desktop app and enter your Harvard SSO email.

![Animated screen recording: signing in to the Claude desktop app with a Harvard SSO email](https://files.slack.com/files-pri/T0HTW3H0V-F0B6SAFG1PU/screenrecording2026-05-28at11.19.33am-ezgif.com-video-to-gif-converter.gif?pub_secret=9b1da884a6)

2. Once signed in, confirm in the bottom-left of the sidebar that your workspace is set to the Harvard Edu instance. If a different workspace is showing, click the workspace name to switch.

![Claude desktop app with the Harvard Edu workspace selected in the lower-left of the sidebar](https://files.slack.com/files-pri/T0HTW3H0V-F0B7LJACN64/claude-app.png?pub_secret=0c1433b40a)
